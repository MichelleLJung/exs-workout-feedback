What to do

  1. Choose a workout from the schedule below. Each workout lasts 45 minutes.
  2. Check in before the workout at the front desk outside the Human Performance Lab (HW-828). Enter your name and an email address you can access.
  3. Participate in the full workout in the HW Movement Lab. Follow the teaching team’s directions and use an appropriate exercise option when needed.
  4. Complete the emailed evaluation afterward. Submit all four ratings and both short answers. Your feedback helps the student teaching team improve.
  5. Submit your attendance details in this Canvas assignment using the instructions below.
