# Resource footer standard

Use this standard for existing and new interactive pages unless Michelle explicitly requests a change.

- Keep the visible creator/licensing footer to one quiet line: “© 2026 Michelle L. Jung · About this tool”. Use the shared resource-about.css and resource-about.js.
- Use small readable text (12px), adequate contrast, a keyboard-accessible link, and a minimum 24px link target. Let the footer wrap on narrow screens; do not use a fixed or sticky footer.
- Open the AI-development acknowledgment only on request in the shared native dialog. Keep the full licensing and educational disclaimer at licensing.html, with a working non-JavaScript fallback from the About link.
- Preserve the existing CC BY 4.0 content / MIT code terms and third-party exclusions. Do not add large copyright boxes, repeated licensing paragraphs, or AI notices throughout the activity.
- The acknowledgment is: “Created by Michelle L. Jung with generative AI assistance in design, content development, and coding. I review and test each activity and verify its instructional content and accuracy before publication.”
- Do not assert that responses stay local or are not sent to AI without checking the specific tool. A future feature that processes user input with AI needs an appropriate notice at the point of use.
- Hide the shared footer and dialog in print. Preserve existing PDF/report credits and output layout; do not add the AI-development paragraph to student reports.
- Keep source citations, activity instructions, data controls, and necessary privacy information in their appropriate locations.
- For footer-only changes, preserve activity logic, saved-work keys, forms, assessment criteria, and exports. Batch changes into one release per site, and check desktop/mobile appearance, keyboard operation, and links before publishing.
